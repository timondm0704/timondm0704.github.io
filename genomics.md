### Parent-of-Origin Effects on Obesity
*Parent-of-Origin Effects (POE) · Framingham Heart Study (FHS) · Obesity · mQTL/vQTL/eQTL*

Does it matter which parent you inherited a gene variant from?<br>
I study whether variants in FTO, a well-established obesity-associated gene, affect obesity differently depending on parental origin, using data from the Framingham Heart Study (FHS).

### Pharmacogenetic GWAS of Bone Mineral Density
*Multi-site randomized controlled clinical trial · HPC pipeline · Gene-Treatment Interaction · Osteoporosis · Bone Mineral Density · Statistical Genetics*

Can your genes predict how your bone respond to a medication? <br>
I run a genome-wide association study (GWAS) in the Atenolol for the Prevention of Osteoporosis (APO) trial, a multi-site randomized clinical trial of atenolol (a beta-blocker) versus placebo, to identify genetic variants that change how treatment affects bone mineral density over time.

### Variance Heterogeneity in Metabolic Traits: A Chromosome Substitution Strain (CSS) Mouse Study
*Mouse genetics study · Funded by NIH Transformative Research Award · Permutation-based statistics*

Why do genetically identical mice on the same diet end up so different? <br>
As part of a multi-institution research team, I implement the statistical methods and visualizations for a study of genetically defined mice. The study asks which parts of the genome control not only the average value of a metabolic trait but also how much it varies between individuals. My work tests both mean and variance effects across strains, diets, sexes, and a full panel of chromosome substitution strains mice.

### BifurcatoR: Detecting Variance Heterogeneity
*Variance Heterogeneity · Bimodality · Statistical Power · Simulation Study · Phenotypic Noise · Variability*

Why do patients with the same diagnosis respond so differently to the same treatment, and which statistical tests can reliably find the hidden subgroups behind that difference?<br>
I implemented a large-scale simulation study benchmarking 17 statistical methods for detecting variance differences and bimodality across thousands of data scenarios. The results became the evidence-based guidance behind BifucatoR, an open-source R package and web app that helps researchers find clinically meaningful patient subgroups.
