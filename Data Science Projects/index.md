### Flight Delay Prediction: Is Bad Weather Really to Blame?

*PySpark · Spark MLlib · Classification · Feature Selection · Cross-Validation*

Airlines often blame late departures on the weather, but is that the whole story?
I built an end-to-end PySpark pipeline on 4.5 million U.S. flights, comparing logistic regression, SVM, and decision tree models on weather-only and operations-only features, and found that weather alone explains surprisingly little.
